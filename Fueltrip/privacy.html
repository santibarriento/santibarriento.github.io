Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. Information We Collect

If you use Google Sign-In, we may collect your name, email address, and profile picture provided by Google.

Fueltrip may request access to your precise location through GPS to find nearby gas stations and calculate travel routes. This information is processed on your device and may be sent to Google Maps services to provide these features.

You may also save vehicle information, such as make, model, and fuel consumption. This information is stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information to:

  • Provide accurate fuel cost calculations.
  • Personalize the application according to your preferences.
  • Manage Pro purchases and subscriptions through Google Play.
  • Display relevant advertisements in the free version.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

Fueltrip may integrate with the following services:

  • Google Play Services: maps and location features.
  • Firebase: authentication and crash reporting.
  • AdMob: advertising services.
  • Google Play Billing: secure payment processing.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

4. Security and Data Sharing

Personal information transmitted through Fueltrip is protected using secure communication protocols. We do not sell your personal information or share it with third parties for unrelated commercial purposes.

Information may be disclosed when necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, or protect our rights.

5. Data Retention and Control

You can delete locally stored data by clearing the application data or uninstalling Fueltrip. If you signed in with Google, you can sign out from the Fueltrip settings to disconnect your Firebase account.

6. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to the processing of your personal information, as well as data portability where applicable.

To exercise your rights or ask questions, contact us at studios.stb@gmail.com. You may also file a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect legal, technical, or functional changes. The current version will always be available on this page.
